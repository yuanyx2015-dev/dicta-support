Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026. This policy describes DICTA — Learn Latin Through Famous Sayings.

Contact the DICTA developer at alexyuan2017@gmail.com.

What stays on this device

If you play as a guest, your progress, sound and motion settings, nickname when you set one, and a marker that you have finished the opening film stay on this device. Guest progress is not uploaded. Clearing the app, or using Delete all information in Settings, removes it.

If you continue with Apple or Google

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and the Apple ID or Google account you choose. DICTA receives the identity that sign-in provides, including the email address, so the game can open your own save. Apple may hide the address behind a private relay address. DICTA does not ask for permission to read your mail, and it does not use your Apple or Google profile name as your public nickname.

The nickname you choose is saved with your account. It stays private unless you separately agree, in the Hall of Heroes, to show a public name.

Your campaign progress is stored in Cloud Firestore for the dicta-latin project, in a document only your signed-in account can read. A copy is also kept on this device so you can keep playing when the network is unavailable.

Hall of Heroes

Joining is optional. If you agree, other players can see the nickname you chose, your hint count, your battle time, and the completion time. They cannot see your email address. You can withdraw the public listing in the Hall. Withdrawal removes the public name. Delete all information also removes the private records behind it.

What DICTA does not do

DICTA does not sell personal information, show advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps. The Latin voice in the game is a synthetic recording stored with the app. It is not a recording of your voice.

Delete all information

Open Settings and choose Delete all information. This is separate from Reset Learning Progress. Reset starts a new campaign and keeps completion memories and first Challenge records. Delete all information removes the DICTA information on this device. If you are signed in, it also deletes the cloud save, the nickname, Hall of Heroes entries, private Challenge records, and the Apple or Google sign-in DICTA stored. DICTA asks you to sign in once more so that connection can be removed. Your Apple ID and Google Account are not closed.

The button asks you to type DELETE ALL. If the server cannot finish the account deletion, DICTA stops and leaves the copy on this device in place. Nothing is removed halfway without telling you.

You can also email alexyuan2017@gmail.com to ask for account deletion.

Children

DICTA is a Latin learning game. It is not directed at collecting information from children beyond the guest save on the device or, if someone signs in, the Apple or Google account and chosen nickname described above.
