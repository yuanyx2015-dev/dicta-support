Support

DICTA is an iPad app for learning Latin through famous sayings. You can play as a guest, or sign in with Apple or Google and keep your own save.

Ask for help

Email alexyuan2017@gmail.com. Say what you were doing, and include the iPad model if the app misbehaved.

Delete your information

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all information. The app asks you to type DELETE ALL. That removes the DICTA information on the device. If you are signed in, it also deletes the cloud save, the nickname, Hall of Heroes entries, private Challenge records, and the Apple or Google sign-in stored by DICTA. Your Apple ID and Google Account stay open.

You can also email alexyuan2017@gmail.com and ask for the account to be deleted.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes what stays on the device, what a signed-in save stores, and the optional Hall of Heroes.
